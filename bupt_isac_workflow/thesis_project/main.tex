% 中文摘要
\chapter*{摘\quad 要}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{摘要}

随着低空经济上升为国家战略性新兴产业，低空智联网（LA-IoT）作为保障海量低空飞行器安全巡航、协同调度与数据交互的关键数字化基础设施，其网络感知与通信能力面临着前所未有的严苛挑战。低空空域飞行器具有低空、慢速、小雷达截面等特征，加之复杂低空环境下的严重建筑物物理遮挡与高动态空地多普勒扩展，传统分立的通信基站与雷达站架构面临部署成本高昂、空域盲区广泛以及频谱冲突加剧的现实瓶颈。

通感一体化（ISAC）技术通过频谱共享、硬件复用与基带联合信号处理，为低空智联网构建了全域感知与高效传输深度融合的新途径。针对低空复杂信道环境下的通感资源竞争、强多普勒扩展以及空间非凸轨迹耦合问题，本文深入开展面向低空无人机系统的通感一体化波形优化与感知先验辅助资源管理技术研究，主要研究成果包括：
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{构建空地多载波通感一体化全维度系统模型}：结合低空几何多径信道、莱斯衰落与飞行运动学特征，形式化推导出多用户下行通信信干噪比与雷达目标多参数估计的克拉美罗下界（CRB）闭式矩阵。
  \item \textbf{提出基于 CRB 与互信息量的 Pareto 最优波形预编码设计架构}：揭示了双功能波形在正交频域子载波上的功率分配规律，通过半正定松弛（SDR）与矩阵提升技术将非凸波形设计转化为易于求解的凸近似子问题。
  \item \textbf{建立感知先验引导的无人机动态轨迹与主动波束跟踪机制}：利用雷达回波扩展卡尔曼滤波输出的后验运动学状态，大幅压缩通信波束对准搜索空间，在满足无人机物理动力学与空间防撞硬约束下实现了通感协同效用最大化。
  \item \textbf{设计基于 SCA-BCD 的低复杂度交替迭代求解算法并完成理论收敛性证明}：在数学上严格证明了算法目标函数序列的单调非减性，分析了多项式计算复杂度边界，并建立了标准化学术脚手架评测基准。
\end{enumerate}

\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

Driven by the strategic expansion of the low-altitude economy, the Low-Altitude Internet of Things (LA-IoT) serves as an essential digital foundation to guarantee safe airspace operation, autonomous coordination, and reliable high-rate data transmission for massive unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). However, low-altitude aircraft typically exhibit low-altitude, slow-speed, and small-size (LSS) physical characteristics. Compounded by severe electromagnetic blockage in urban canyons and substantial Doppler shifts induced by high mobility, conventional disjoint communication and radar infrastructures suffer from prohibitive deployment costs, extensive sensing blind zones, and severe spectrum scarcity.

Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) presents a transformative paradigm to overcome these bottlenecks by sharing hardware platforms, radio frequency front-ends, and wireless spectrum resources. To address the critical trade-offs between communication throughput and sensing precision in low-altitude scenarios, this thesis conducts systematic research on joint waveform optimization and sensing-assisted dynamic resource management for UAV-enabled ISAC networks. The main contributions are summarized as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Comprehensive Modeling of Air-to-Ground Multicarrier ISAC Systems}: Integrating three-dimensional spatial geometry, Rician fading channels, and Doppler effects, we derive rigorous closed-form expressions for downlink communication SINR and the estimation Cramér-Rao Bound (CRB) matrix for radar sensing.
  \item \textbf{Pareto-Optimal Waveform Precoding Framework}: We uncover the fundamental power allocation trade-offs across orthogonal subcarriers and employ Semidefinite Relaxation (SDR) with matrix lifting to formulate tractable convex approximations for non-convex waveform synthesis.
  \item \textbf{Sensing-Assisted Proactive Beam Tracking and Dynamic Trajectory Coordination}: Leveraging posterior kinematic state estimates from extended Kalman filtering, we substantially compress the beam alignment overhead and maximize overall network utility under kinematics and collision avoidance constraints.
  \item \textbf{Low-Complexity SCA-BCD Optimization Algorithm with Convergence Guarantee}: We rigorously prove that the generated objective function sequence converges monotonically to a stationary point satisfying first-order KKT optimality conditions, establish polynomial complexity bounds, and construct a standardized scaffold benchmark.
\end{enumerate}

\vspace{0.6cm}
\noindent \textbf{Keywords}: Low-Altitude Internet of Things; UAV Communications; Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC); Waveform Design; Cramér-Rao Bound (CRB); Alternating Optimization
